Glitch Project

Glitch Art, 2026. Medium/tools: Text editor used to data bend.

Description: This project uses distortion, compression, and digital failure as aesthetic language. I chose to glitch an album cover: King by a band called Belly. I provided a screenshot to stabilize the result, just so I can show others what I saw when I edited it. I performed duplication and deletion edits around the 10% mark. I inserted a handful of random, short series of numbers into this region as well.
