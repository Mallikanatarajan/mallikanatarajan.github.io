Pulling apart clumps of vines, you fight your way through the curtain of leaves.

You find yourself in an... ominously quiet and almost spherical chamber, like a natural dome, way taller than you expected.

There's a large branching tunnel to your left that seems to turn into an area that has a purple light source - the dim glow is almost calling to you.

You dash around the corner and stop dead in your tracks...

In front of you there is a swirling portal to another dimension.

Step through the purple haze
