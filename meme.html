Meme

Meme, 2026. Medium/tools: Adobe Photoshop.

This is an ironic internet culture themed mashup of FL studio memes, Spongebob, a youtube thumbnail and a popular reaction image to simulate the feeling of consuming internet maximalism to the point that blends together. You might encounter this dopamine-flooding feeling scrolling through funny ironic TikToks or messaging in a popular Discord server. I became interested in the subject because I encountered media like this for myself, and as I kept scrolling and refining my algorithm, I started to see it more often.

I sourced media from screenshots I took of memes on my phone of reels I had watched and memes I had saved, a youtube thumbnail I found very corny, which I thought fit the irony aesthetic, and emojis. I masked out a button that says FOENEM from one of my screenshots and pasted it all over the image, a reference to the phrase "on foenem". And of course Spongebob is very recognizable, but I also chose to include Spongebob characters because the show is also a very common subject to see mashed up in ironic tiktoks, sometimes even dubbed over with absurd dialog.

This type of ironic video can almost be labeled an aesthetic in itself, and it inspired me to mash up elements in an image to imitate how it was done in these short form videos. I enjoyed trying to document replicate an existing online aesthetic - I might even consider this one "deeply online" because often understanding these kinds of videos for what they are and finding humor in them rather than being confused by the visual clutter needs some deep internet context. This mashup exposes those who have not encountered this internet niche to this specific brand of internet irony.
